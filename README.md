# FatherFirst — Product Strategy & Behavioral Engagement Case Study

FatherFirst is a male fertility health platform concept designed to support shared responsibility in early pregnancy preparation.  
This case study explores product strategy, behavioral engagement loops, and cohort retention modeling to drive long-term habit consistency.

---

# Problem Statement

Male reproductive health significantly impacts:
- Conception success rates
- Miscarriage risk
- Early fetal development

Yet:
- Social stigma discourages open discussion
- Men lack clear guidance for improving sperm health
- Lifestyle changes influencing sperm quality take ~90 days to show impact

This results in an unequal emotional and physical burden during the pregnancy journey.

---

# Target Persona

Men 25–38, preparing for future parenthood, who say:

“I want to play my part in ensuring a healthy pregnancy, not leave everything to my partner.”

Pain Points:
- Doesn't know where to begin
- Overwhelmed by conflicting online information
- Wants support, not guilt or pressure
---

## Key Insights

Male reproductive health support is not just about fertility outcomes:
It is about **care, preparation, and responsibility in partnership.**

When health behaviors are framed as:
- Preparation
- Care
- Support

Men are more likely to adopt and sustain habits.

---

## Onboarding Journey

1. Welcome — Shared responsibility message
2. 6-Question Lifestyle Check-In
3. At-Home Fertility Test Kit (biomarkers)
4. 90-Day Micro-Habit Plan (based on sperm development cycles)
5. 1-Minute Daily Reflection Check-In
